BIRTHDAY SURPRISE WEBSITE — WITH YOUR PHOTOS

Files:
- index.html
- style.css
- script.js
- photos/ (8 photos bundled into the site)

To test:
1. Keep the whole extracted folder together.
2. Open index.html in a browser.
3. Go through the pages; all 8 photos appear automatically on the Photos page.
4. Tap any photo to open the larger slideshow. Use arrows or keyboard arrows to browse.

To share it online:
Upload the ENTIRE folder contents (including the photos folder) to your static website host.
If you already have a hosted website, upload/replace index.html, style.css, script.js and the photos folder.
Keep birthday-song.mp3 in the same main folder if you want the song to play.
